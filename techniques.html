Core Prompting Techniques

Zero-Shot Prompting

Asking the model to perform a task without any prior examples. The model relies solely on its pre-trained knowledge.

Few-Shot Prompting

Providing a few examples (shots) in the prompt to show the model the desired format or style of the response.

Chain-of-Thought (CoT)

Encouraging the model to explain its reasoning process step-by-step before arriving at a final answer. This significantly improves performance on complex tasks.
